About

BOI Pro Auto (also listed as BOI PRO AUTO LLC) is an automotive repair shop at 4313 Broadway Avenue J in Galveston. Google reviews mention mechanical and electrical work, including engines, air conditioning, wipers, wheel sensors, a power steering pump, an alternator, paint, and headlight polishing. Reviewers name a manager, Pepe, and a team member, Eloy.

Google hours are 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Monday through Friday and 8 a.m.–2 p.m. Saturday, closed Sunday. The shop's public web presence is a Linktree and a Facebook page; no owned website was found. (A different Galveston business, Canales Auto Deals, sometimes appears in nearby directory results and is not this shop.)
