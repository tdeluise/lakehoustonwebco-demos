About

Legacy Barbershop is at 426 N Main St in downtown Conroe. Public reviews mention fades, straight-razor work around the ears and neck, shaves, men's and women's cuts, kids' cuts, and barbers named Joshua and Sal. Google lists a 4.8 rating from 35 reviews; some older reviews are critical, and that rating is shown as published.

Google hours are closed Monday and Sunday, and 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Tuesday through Saturday. No owned website was found; the shop uses Facebook, a public email listed on its public profile, and a phone number.
