About

Maggie's Taqueria is at 1311 S Texas Ave in Bryan. Public reviews mention breakfast tacos, including chorizo and egg and potato tacos, barbacoa, menudo, fresh tortillas, and quesadillas. Google lists a 4.3 rating from 39 reviews; some reviews are critical, and that rating is shown as published. Several of the reviews found here are from 2017–2019, with later comments as recent as November 2024, so this preview does not treat that as a large 2025–2026 review history.

Google hours are 6 a.m.–3 p.m. Monday through Friday, 6 a.m.–1 p.m. Saturday, and closed Sunday. No owned website was found. chow.com is a directory profile, not an owned domain. The restaurant uses Facebook and a phone number.
