About

Nu Era Barbershop (also listed as Nu-Era Barber Shop) is at 3533 S Dairy Ashford Rd, Suite C, in Houston's Alief area. Public reviews mention haircuts, beard trims, kids' cuts, and a barber and owner named Shawn. Another review from April 2025 names a barber, Jermaine, who cut a 10-year-old's hair.

Google hours are 9:30 a.m.–7 p.m. Tuesday through Saturday, 9:30 a.m.–4 p.m. Sunday, and closed Monday. One directory listed Sunday closing at noon; this preview uses the Google hours and asks you to call if you are coming on Sunday. No owned website was found.
