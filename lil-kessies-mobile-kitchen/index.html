About

Lil Kessie's Mobile Kitchen is a food truck at 1989 N Fry Rd in Katy. Public reviews mention Louisiana-style and Houston soul food, including oxtails, fried fish with étouffée, crawfish étouffée, chicken and sausage okra, pork steak sandwiches, ghost pepper and lemon pepper wings, greens, yams, mac and cheese, red beans and rice, cornbread, and a homemade hot link, along with large portions and friendly service. Reviews refer to the operator in different ways, so this preview does not assume a name or gender.

Google hours are Thursday 5–9 p.m., Friday 5–11 p.m., Saturday 3–11 p.m., and Sunday 3–9 p.m., with Monday through Wednesday closed. No owned website was found; the truck uses Facebook, a public email, and a phone number.
