Before you book

A few policies that keep the schedule fair for every client.

Deposits

A non-refundable 50% deposit, or one session from a package, is required to secure your appointment.

Cancelling or rescheduling

Give at least 24 hours’ notice to keep your deposit. You can reschedule once before your deposit is forfeited.

Running late

There’s a 15-minute grace period. After that, a $10 late fee applies, and your appointment may need to be rescheduled out of respect for the next client.

No-shows

Missed appointments are charged 100% of the service.

Guests

Children under 13 and extra guests who aren’t being serviced can’t join you in the suite.
