About

Taqueria Aquilar is at 2222 Texas Ave in College Station. Public reviews mention al pastor and asada tacos, a grill-meat and sausage combo with rice and beans, and green salsa. Google lists a 5.0 rating from 3 reviews, which is a very small sample, so this preview does not treat that as a large review history.

Public hours and a phone number were not found on the listing used for this preview, so it is best to check Facebook or visit in person. No owned website was found; the restaurant uses Facebook, a public email, and a physical address.
