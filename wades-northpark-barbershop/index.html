A no-nonsense Kingwood shop

Wade’s Northpark Barbershop sits at 3054 Northpark Drive in Kingwood. Google also lists it as Northpark Barber Shop. Reviewers come back for Wade’s cuts — some for decades — and for a laid-back, old-school room.

One Google review mentions a fellow barber, Robbie, and the Astrodome seats in the waiting area. Another regular wrote that Wade has been cutting his hair for over 25 years.

In the chair

Services named in public reviews. No official menu was found.

Haircuts & fades

Reviewers mention everyday cuts, simple fades, and being in and out quickly.

Flattops & high and tights

Clients single out Wade for flattops, including a USMC horseshoe flattop, and a “high and tight done right.”

Straight-razor shaves & beard trims

A visiting barber praised Robbie’s straight-razor shave. Others mention beard trims done with care.

Custom work

One family review describes designs, a high and tight pompadour, and an undercut — “every time [we] come in, we experiment!”
