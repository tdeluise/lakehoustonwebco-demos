About

Tomball Barber Shop is at 613 W Main St in Tomball. Public reviews name Paul and his wife as the barbers, mention cuts for kids and a clean fade finished with a straight razor, and describe a shop that has served the town for decades. Some reviews are mixed, so this preview does not claim every request is accepted.

Google hours are 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Tuesday through Friday, 8 a.m.–3 p.m. Saturday, and closed Sunday and Monday. One older review said a listing showed 6 p.m.; this preview uses the current Google hours. No owned website was found. (A physical-therapy domain sometimes appears next to this listing in directories and is not this shop.)

What's offered

  • Haircuts, including children's cuts mentioned in reviews
  • Fades, including a straight-razor finish mentioned in reviews
  • Walk-ins / no appointments mentioned in reviews

This list comes only from public listings and reviews. Ask the shop to confirm current offerings and prices.
