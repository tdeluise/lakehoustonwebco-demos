About

Telge Donuts is a donut shop at 14030 Telge Rd, Suite D, in Cypress. Public reviews mention glazed and old-fashioned donuts, blueberry donuts, apple fritters, decorated kids' donuts (including Elmo and Cookie Monster designs), kolaches, croissants, breakfast sandwiches, and breakfast tacos, and they describe friendly counter service and custom orders.

Google hours are 5 a.m.–1 p.m. every day. No owned website was found; the shop uses Facebook, a public email, and a SinglePlatform menu listing.
