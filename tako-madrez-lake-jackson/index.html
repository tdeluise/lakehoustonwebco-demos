About

Tako Madrez is a food truck at 115 Plantation Dr in Lake Jackson. Public reviews mention birria, birria quesadillas, jalapeño tacos, asada tacos, burritos, beans, nachos, agua fresca, and tres leches cake. Google lists a 4.6 rating from 78 reviews; some reviews mention arriving to find the truck closed, and that rating is shown as published.

Google hours are 10 a.m.–9 p.m. Monday through Thursday, 10 a.m.–11 p.m. Friday, 11 a.m.–7 p.m. Saturday, and closed Sunday. A December 2025 review said Facebook listed later evening hours and that the truck was sometimes closed in the afternoon, so it is best to call or check ahead. No owned website was found; the truck uses Facebook, a public email, and a phone number.

What's offered

  • Birria and birria quesadillas mentioned in reviews
  • Street tacos, including jalapeño and asada tacos mentioned in reviews
  • Burritos, beans, and nachos mentioned in reviews
  • Agua fresca and tres leches cake mentioned in reviews

This list comes only from public listings and reviews. Ask the shop to confirm current offerings and prices.
