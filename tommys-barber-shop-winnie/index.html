About

Tommy's Barber Shop is at 215 W Walnut Ave in Winnie. The Better Business Bureau lists Michael Crainer as the owner, and Google reviews also name Michael (sometimes spelled Micheal or Mike) as the barber. Reviewers mention regular haircuts, a high-and-tight, a mullet, head shaves, and cuts for children, and some say they drive a long way to get here. A longtime customer wrote that Tommy started the shop and that Mike is the barber they see now. BBB also lists the business as starting in 1991.

Google hours are 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Wednesday through Friday, 8 a.m.–3 p.m. Saturday, and closed Sunday through Tuesday. No owned website was found.
