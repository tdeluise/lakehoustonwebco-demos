About

Delia's Signature Bakery is a bakery at 28 Harold Lane in Baytown. Public listings and reviews describe breakfast tacos, lunch-style tacos, pastries, drinks, and Mexican sweet bread, and several reviewers mention a drive-through window and a friendly person at the window.

Google hours show the bakery open 5 a.m. to noon Monday through Saturday and closed Sunday. Directory copy has called it an unassuming Mexican bakery offering sweets plus savory items such as tacos and canastitas.
