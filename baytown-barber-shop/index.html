About

Baytown Barber Shop is at 4810 N Main St in Baytown. Public reviews mention barbers named Matt (including Matt Crockett), Rudy, Kevin, Robert, and Manny, and they describe haircuts for kids and adults, plus straight razors, hot towels, and brow trimming in at least one review. The Google rating is 4.4 from 107 reviews, and some reviews are mixed, so this preview does not claim every visit is the same.

Google hours are 9 a.m.–6 p.m. Tuesday through Friday, 9 a.m.–3 p.m. Saturday, and closed Sunday and Monday. No owned website was found.
