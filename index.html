Questions

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours. If you ever want to move it somewhere else, you can.

Why is there no monthly fee?

Your site is a simple, fast page that doesn't need a paid hosting plan. The only ongoing cost is your domain, about $12 a year, and that's paid in your name.

What do you need from me to start?

Nothing. I build the preview from what's already public. If you like it, send me any photos or menu changes you want on it.

What if I don't like the preview?

Then you don't owe anything. The preview is free either way.

I already have a Facebook page. Do I need this?

Keep your Facebook page. Your site links to it, and it gives people who search your name a real page with your hours, phone number and address.

Can I change things later?

Small text changes like hours or prices are free for the first 30 days after launch. After that, just ask.
