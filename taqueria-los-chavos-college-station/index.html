About

Taqueria Los Chavos is at 1800 Harvey Mitchell Pkwy S in College Station. Public reviews mention a food truck serving birria tortas, quesabirria, gorditas, breakfast tacos, and fajita tacos. Some listings also mention a second location; this preview is only for the Harvey Mitchell Parkway shop. Google lists a 4.7 rating from 75 reviews; some reviews are mixed or critical, and that rating is shown as published.

Google hours are 7 a.m.–2 p.m. Monday through Saturday and closed Sunday. Popular-times data on the same listing also shows evening activity, so hours may differ from that posted range, and it is best to call ahead. No owned website was found. DoorDash is an ordering page, not an owned domain. A public Facebook page was not found on the listing used here.
