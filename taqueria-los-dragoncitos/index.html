About

Taqueria los Dragoncitos is at 1522 S Texas Ave in Bryan. Public reviews mention a food truck or trailer serving tacos, including pastor and campechana, gorditas, hamburgers, hot dogs, tortas, alambre, carnitas, complementary potatoes, and aguas frescas. A January 2025 review named the owner as Karina; that first name is included as published in a review, not as a confirmed legal owner. A 2023 review said the truck sometimes changes locations, so it is best to call ahead. Google lists a 4.2 rating from 28 reviews; some reviews are critical, and that rating is shown as published.

Google hours are closed Monday, 4 p.m.–11 p.m. Tuesday through Thursday, and 3:30 p.m.–12 a.m. Friday through Sunday. No owned website was found; the truck uses Facebook, a public email, and a phone number.
