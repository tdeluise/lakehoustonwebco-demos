About

Nellys Taqueria is at 901 N Earl Rudder Fwy in Bryan. Public reviews describe a taco trailer near a Valero, at the corner of William J Bryan and the Highway 6 bypass, and mention breakfast tacos, street tacos including al pastor, homemade tortillas, and fajita tacos with rice and beans. Google lists a 4.7 rating from 23 reviews.

Google hours are 6 a.m.–2 p.m. Monday through Saturday and closed Sunday. No owned website was found; DoorDash is an ordering page, and a negocio.site address returned 404 on Nov 16, 2026. The trailer uses Facebook, a public email, and a phone number.
