About

BADOM Barbershop (also listed as BaDom Barber Shop) is at 11326 Westheimer Rd, Suite D, in Houston's Westchase / Briar Forest area. Public booking on Booksy lists haircuts, beard work, kids' haircuts, beard shaping, and lineups. Open review snippets mention fades and a welcoming shop; one reviewer said they drive an hour so their son can get a cut here. A barber named Eduar (Daniel) is mentioned in a public directory summary.

Google hours are 9 a.m.–8 p.m. Monday through Thursday, 9 a.m.–9 p.m. Friday and Saturday, and 10 a.m.–4 p.m. Sunday. No owned website was found — only Facebook, Booksy, and directory listings.
