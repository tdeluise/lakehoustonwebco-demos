About

Chepo's is a food truck in Pasadena. Google/Exa list the address as 2701 Shaver St; Yelp lists 2502 Shaver St. Please call or check Facebook for the exact location. Reviews mention tacos, tortas (including a mega torta), piratas, pastor, pierna, bistec, charro beans, homemade fries or papas preparadas, and a Sunday special, and they describe a family-run truck.

Google hours are 6–11 p.m. Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Sunday, and 6 p.m.–2 a.m. Friday and Saturday, with Monday closed. No owned website was found.

What's offered

  • Tacos mentioned in reviews
  • Tortas, including a mega torta mentioned in reviews
  • Piratas mentioned in reviews
  • Pastor, pierna, and bistec mentioned in reviews
  • Charro beans and homemade fries / papas preparadas mentioned in reviews
  • A Sunday special mentioned in reviews
