About

Longhorn Chicken and Burgers is a food truck listed at 14695 TX-105 in Cleveland, near Tarkington High School according to a public review. Reviews mention burgers made with fresh (not frozen) beef, French fries, wings, snack tacos, hot dogs, and nachos, and they describe a clean trailer.
