About

Bonnie's Donut (also listed as Bonnie's Donuts) is a family-owned bakery at 2047 W Main St, Suite A4, in League City. Public reviews describe donuts, kolaches, cronuts, croissant kolaches, fritters, and large cinnamon rolls, and several visitors mention being offered samples and talking with the owners.

Google listings show the shop closed on Mondays and opening at 5 a.m. the rest of the week. One December 2025 review said they were open on Christmas Day; hours can change, so call ahead for holiday openings.

What's offered

  • Donuts, including cake donuts and cronuts mentioned in reviews
  • Kolaches, including bacon-wrapped croissant kolaches mentioned in reviews
  • Cinnamon rolls, including large rolls that some reviewers said can be reserved or prepared on request
  • Donut holes, including ube, blueberry, lemon, and chocolate cake holes mentioned in reviews
  • Fritters and croissants mentioned in reviews
  • Matcha croissant donut and king cake cinnamon rolls mentioned in reviews

This list comes only from public listings and reviews. Ask the shop to confirm current offerings and prices.
